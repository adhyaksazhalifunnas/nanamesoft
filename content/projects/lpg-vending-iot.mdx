## Context

In 2023 the Indonesian Ministry of Energy tightened how subsidised 3 kg LPG
reaches people. Ministerial Decree 37.K/MG.01/MEM.M/2023 made the subsidy
conditional on registration: the buyer presents a family card and an electronic
ID, and the depot checks them against Pertamina's web application before
selling. The intent is sound — the subsidy is meant for households, micro
businesses, fishers and farmers, and it was leaking to people who did not qualify.

The cost landed on the depots. Most are not standalone businesses; they are a
counter attached to a shop, run by one or two people. The new flow means
stopping, taking the customer's card, opening a website, checking, and only then
selling a cylinder — per transaction. Sales slow down, and the operator's time
goes to verification rather than to the rest of their business.

There was a second problem the depot owners we spoke to raised themselves. A
leaking cylinder is gas that cannot be sold, and in the worst case it is a fire.
Nobody is monitoring for it, particularly outside trading hours.

Our capstone brief was to prototype something that addressed both: a vending
machine that does the eligibility check itself, handles both 3 kg and 5.5 kg
cylinders so the depot is not limited to subsidised sales, and watches its own
interior for gas.

## Implementation

The machine is an Arduino Uno coordinating three sensing paths and two
actuator paths, and the design principle throughout is that the safety path never
depends on the network.

**Identification.** An RFID reader takes the chip in the buyer's e-KTP and
resolves it to one of three roles held on the device: machine operator,
registered subsidised buyer, or general buyer. The LCD on the front reports what
was read. The measured read range is about 1.5 cm — cards simply do not register
at 2 cm — and a read at 1 cm averages 1,320 ms across four different tag types,
dropping to 800 ms in contact. That range is short enough that it has to be
designed for: the card is tapped, not waved.

**Classification.** With the role established, the empty-cylinder door opens and
the TCS3200 reads the inserted cylinder's colour. Indonesian LPG cylinders are
already colour-coded by size, so the RGB reading maps to a type, an LED confirms
it to the buyer, and the correct internal door opens. Accuracy averaged 92.7%
across distances from 1.5 to 5.5 cm, and we tested it against objects in
deliberately similar colours to find where it confuses them.

**Transport.** Servo-driven doors and a conveyor move the cylinder along its
route inside the machine, with the route selected by the classification result.

**Safety.** The MQ6 samples continuously. Above threshold, three things happen in
order: the buzzer and LED fire locally, a WhatsApp message goes to the owner via
Twilio, and the machine powers down. The ordering is the decision. The local
alarm fired on 100% of test events and the sensor's own ADC readings tracked a
multimeter to within 0.97%, while only four of five WhatsApp alerts arrived —
send delay around 3 s, receipt around 1.75 s. A safety response whose reliability
is 80% is not a safety response, so the notification is the convenience layer and
the shutdown is the safeguard.

Leading a team of four across three study programmes meant the interfaces
mattered more than the code. The electrical and biomedical engineering members
owned sensing and mechanics; the information technology side owned the control
logic and the alerting path. Defining what each sensor had to hand the Arduino,
early and in writing, is what let those parts be built in parallel and still fit
together.
